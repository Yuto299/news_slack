**1. [Nscale、Anthropicと450億ドル規模の追加計算資源契約を協議中と報道](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/nscale-and-anthropic-are-reportedly-in-talks-for-yet-another-45-billion-compute-deal/)**

**4. [CoreWeaveの社債が「ディストレス」水準に、AI業界全体のリスクに](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1014045/coreweave-stock-debt-sector-ai)**
